Topic workspace prototype

Explore a project, open a topic, drill into a subtopic, and read its materials. Topic cards, Markdown previews and AI context share one synthetic state. Reload resets edits; no research files or model are connected.

Synthetic content · no files are read or written

Try the research-state scenarios

  1. Change the objective: open the topic, edit Question and scope, review the diff, Apply.
  2. Correct an interpretation: open Claims & routes; on the imported result, choose Correct.
  3. Reject a hypothesis: under Claims & routes, on the agent's hypothesis, choose Decline.
  4. Change a route: open Work & choices; on Frequency dependence, choose Supersede by.
  5. Invalidate an assumption: under Claims & routes, flag Applicability condition as invalid and review its dependents.
  6. Update evidence: under Work & choices, on the benchmark pointer, choose Record result.

See the research, then drill into it

  • Ctrl K opens the research palette; type part of a topic, thread or assumption.
  • Ctrl Shift O opens the outline over the page; pin it on wide screens.
  • The sidebar switches between Topics and Conversations in one column. Cards and breadcrumbs keep the route visible.
  • Overview shows subtopics; Work, Claims, Materials and Activity open their details.

See the loop with the agent

  • On any card, Ask agent opens the conversation with a scoped draft; the topic stays in the dock.
  • Send the draft for Challenge assumptions: the agent's proposal appears under Needs you.
  • Apply a change while a Session is open: that Session shows what changed before its next request.
  • Preview an objective edit, then Simulate ▾ an external edit before Apply to see a conflict.
